16–18 September 2026

Speke Resort Munyonyo, Kampala, Uganda

About the Expo

The Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026 is a major coffee industry event organised by the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF). The event brings together coffee farmers, processors, exporters, investors and other stakeholders in the coffee industry to explore opportunities within the global coffee market.

The three-day exhibition will provide opportunities for networking, knowledge sharing, product exhibitions and discussions about modern coffee production, processing technologies, quality improvement and international trade. Participants will also interact with industry experts and discover innovations shaping the future of Uganda's coffee sector.
